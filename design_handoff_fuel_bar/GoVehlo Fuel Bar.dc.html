En brændstofstatus, man forstår på et blik

The flat bar and floating red dot become a proper dial gauge with a needle. The rækkevidde i km leads, with %/liter as quiet support. Both directions cover the whole range — Tank nu (near-empty · healthy · full) and the Tank efter turen projection on the Tur tab. Amber is reserved for money, so a low tank reads in error-red (#D95050), never amber.

Ny — kompakt (anbefalet): the gauge was eating the fold and taxing every visit with a scroll. On a data-entry screen the fuel level is context, not the hero. This slim strip (~50px vs ~150px) keeps km-first and the red/green logic, but the Tur/Tank toggle and the km fields now sit above the fold. Tap to expand into the full gauge (turn 1) when you want detail.
