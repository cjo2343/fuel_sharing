GoVehlo · Bilen — car details redesign

Fra registret → færdig bilprofil

The raw detail page becomes a guided profile: what the nummerplade lookup filled is clearly marked Fra registret, and the four things only the owner can know are surfaced as needs-input tasks — tankstørrelse, forsikringsdetaljer, ejerafgift som fast udgift, and tankstatus. The chosen layout is the split-tracks design (B1 after lookup, B2 complete), with the four edit sheets below.

Direction A — guided completion

One scroll, guided by a completion card

A completion card leads the screen and lists exactly what's missing; every section below carries a Fra registret / Tilføjet af dig tag, and unfilled fields become a forest "Angiv/Tilføj" row in place. When everything's done the card flips to the calm green status.

Bilen — chosen design (split tracks)

Two tracks: review the auto data, do your part

A two-track band splits the profile into Fra registret (already done, trust it) and Skal udfyldes (your job). All the work lives in one "Skal udfyldes af dig" card up top; the sections below stay calm — empty fields read "Ikke angivet" rather than shouting a CTA in every section. Syn shows next inspection + last result from the registry; Reparationshistorik is an owner-logged, splittable expense log — empty in B1, populated in B2.

Field provenance & completion spec

What the nummerplade lookup fills vs what the owner must supply. The profile is "complete" when all four Skal udfyldes rows are done.

Field
Source
Behaviour

Fra registret

Tankstørrelse

Forsikring — dækning · fornyelse · præmie · selvrisiko
Skal udfyldes
User adds all four; præmie & selvrisiko render in amber mono (money) (S2).
Tankstatus — niveau + km nu
Skal angives
User sets fuel level + current odometer; drives the rækkevidde estimate (S3).
Reparationshistorik
Tilføjet af dig
Owner logs each repair (dato · værksted · km · beløb); beløb renders in amber mono and can be split as a shared expense (S5).

Why tankstatus is manual · the car has been driven since the last syn, so neither the registry nor the odometer-at-syn reflects the current fuel level. The owner's one-time reading seeds the running estimate, which each logged trip then decrements. Colour · "needs input" affordances use forest (action), never amber — amber stays reserved for money (præmie, selvrisiko, ejerafgift).
